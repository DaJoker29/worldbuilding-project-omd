Pain Tolerance — When using GRIT specifically to remain functional through physical pain, reroll one failed die and keep the new result. This does not apply to generic toughness, lifting, grappling, poison, or exhaustion unless pain itself is the challenge.

Avoid the Crowd — When using MOVES to avoid being noticed or recognized while among a crowd or group of people, reroll one failed die and keep the new result. Requires other people to disappear among; it does not apply to generic sneaking through an empty area.

Occult — When using WITS to identify, interpret, or understand magic, monsters, curses, rituals, supernatural symbols, or unexplained supernatural phenomena, reroll one failed die and keep the new result. Applies only to occult or supernatural knowledge.	

HEX — TRICK: When you score a Critical on an attack, you may use Hex instead of dealing +1 Damage. Fill the target with dread; its next Action before the end of its next turn is +1 Difficulty.

I WROTE SOMETHING ABOUT THIS — Once per game, reveal that the current situation eerily resembles something you previously wrote in your journal. State one detail your story predicted; the GM makes that detail true within the current scene.

WHATEVER. — Fear, intimidation, embarrassment, and magical effects that compel you through fear do not affect you. This does not protect you from charm, deception, mind control, or other emotional effects.
